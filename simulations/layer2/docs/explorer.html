Each dot is an agent that copies the average heading of everyone within one radius, then adds random noise: the Vicsek model behind LIMINAL's Layer 2. Turn the noise dial and watch the flock form, break into clusters, and dissolve. The blue dots stand in for the 8 real drones that will fly among virtual agents (about 120 fit the 4 x 4 m net; larger flocks are shown on screen).

What the order number means

Add up every agent's heading as an arrow of length 1 and divide by the number of agents. If all point the same way the result is 1; if they point at random it shrinks toward zero.

Why the fluctuation peak matters

Near the noise level where order is about to break, the flock swings the most and reacts most strongly to one member turning. Try "Turn one drone" at noise 1.0 and then at 2.5.

Why it needs many agents

With 64 agents the switch from order to disorder is a soft blur. With 4,096 it becomes sharp. That is why LIMINAL studies large flocks in simulation and on an FPGA, while the 8 real drones fly among about 120 virtual agents in the net.
